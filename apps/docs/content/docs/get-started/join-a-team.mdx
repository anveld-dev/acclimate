---
title: Join a team's workspace
description: Sign in and connect your agent to a bundle and channel your team manages.
---

1. Accept the workspace invitation and sign in to the console. If the invitation stays pending, verify the invited email address with the identity provider.
2. Open **Bundles**, choose the bundle and channel, and inspect the files in **Content**. Your workspace administrator can create a connection and issue a content-read credential scoped to that channel.
3. [Install the SDK](/docs/get-started/install) and follow the bundle's app setup recipe. Receive the credential through your team's private secret channel.
4. Use the SDK in your application's job startup and retain the version ID and digest with the job result. [Deliver content to jobs](/docs/guides/deliver-content)
