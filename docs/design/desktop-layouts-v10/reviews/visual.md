Further composition must improve that model rather than spend its blank source area on decoration. The remaining pressure is competition among global/scope/task bands, file-rail control alignment and selected-path context. I inspected actual desktop9 published content, dirty draft, open Add entry menu, release history and evidenced publish dialog, plus current Mira configuration, shared styles and Badge/NativeSelect/DropdownMenu primitives. D's rigid columns spend identity/reason room and invent restore fields. Neither current Restore nor edited revision names is a product capability.

Keep the accessible button name, hover/focus Tooltip, Enter, native search clear, applied-query/count and busy semantics. These refinements share the existing Mira system without recoloring status or introducing navigation, new modes or decoration. No bolder geometry demonstrates task benefit.
